AI-powered NPC worlds

Low comp
EmergingAI nativeHigh retention

Roblox is adding real-time AI NPC infrastructure. Almost no games use it meaningfully yet. A game where NPCs hold real conversations, remember players, and drive story is a wide open lane.

Concept: a mystery town where every NPC has secrets — players interrogate them with actual typed questions to solve a case.

Cozy crafting / life sim

Rising
Grow a Garden signalFemale-skewingUnderserved

Grow a Garden's record-breaking 2025 run proved there's massive demand for non-combat, cozy gameplay. Stardew Valley / Animal Crossing-style loop barely exists on Roblox at quality.

Concept: a shared village where players each own a cottage, grow things, trade, and decorate — no combat, pure vibe economy.

Puzzle / escape room

Low comp
DOORS proved demandCo-op friendlyLow build cost

DOORS showed that procedurally generated puzzle-horror works on Roblox. But straight puzzle/escape-room games with no horror — just clever co-op problem-solving — are surprisingly thin.

Concept: a co-op "heist puzzle" game — players have different info and must communicate to crack the same vault. Procedurally generated each run.

Competitive cooking / crafting

Moderate
Overcooked gapSocialViral potential

There's no quality Overcooked-style game on Roblox. Chaotic co-op cooking/crafting has massive viral clip potential (ideal for TikTok/YouTube) and isn't served by current tycoon games.

Concept: rotating kitchen chaos — 4 players, time pressure, escalating orders. Seasonal menus keep it fresh.

Hyperlocal / cultural RP

Low comp
2025 Roblox trend dataUntapped globallyBrand-friendly

Roblox's own 2025 data showed strong searches for localized roleplay (emergency services in Germany, Thai holidays). Culture-specific RP games barely exist outside of English-language defaults.

Concept: a city RP game built around a specific culture (food stalls, festivals, local jobs) — a "Tokyo Street Life" or "Night Market" sim.

Sports prediction / fan games

Rising
Older audience hookReal-world events tie-inNo clear leader

Real sports IP on Roblox is rare and expensive. But fan-made sports fantasy/prediction games tap real-world calendar events (World Cup, Olympics cycles) and pull in an older, higher-spend demo.

Concept: a fantasy sports draft + mini-game format — draft your team, then play skill mini-games to boost your players' weekly stats.
