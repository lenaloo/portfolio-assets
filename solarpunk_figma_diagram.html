Monetization strategy — cozy principle: sell the look, never the loop
Primary — ~55%
Cosmetic items
Seasonal furniture, rare plant species, building skins, travel vehicles, clothing sets. No gameplay impact. Catalogue grows each season.
55%
Primary — ~25%
Starter / founder game pass
One-time 299–499 Robux. Expanded plot, founder cosmetic title, named starter pet. Drives launch monetisation from engaged early players.
25%
Secondary — ~12%
Seasonal battle pass
6–8 week seasons. Free + premium (299R) tracks. Premium gives exclusive seasonal cosmetics. Shared narrative arc per season.
12%
Secondary — ~8%
Rewarded video ads
Opt-in ads (Roblox 2025 feature) for non-spenders. Award bonus crafting resources or time skips. Monetises the ~80% who never spend Robux.
8%
Hard rules: No XP boosts for sale. No rank acceleration. No pay-to-access towns. No time-limited content without seasonal return. Elders treat all players identically regardless of spend.

Town connection rules
All towns are persistent shared servers — a fix by any player is permanent and visible to all visitors.
Towns degrade slowly over real weeks without active players — maintaining frontier tension.
Restored towns (100%) unlock player-run markets and communal build space.
Two additional Tier 2 towns unlock at Month 2 via live ops expansion.

Content safety
System prompt constrains Elder to game world only. All outputs through Roblox moderation filter before display.
Fallback behaviour
If AI API unavailable: pre-written dialogue tree activates. Player experience degrades gracefully, never breaks.
