How matchmaking works

Town need

Each town broadcasts a "restoration board" — open tasks categorised by speciality and difficulty tier. A broken solar field needs 2 Sparkswrights (rank 3+). A dying watershed needs 1 Tidewarden + 1 Rewinder.

Squad fill

Players queue with their active speciality and rank. The system builds squads of 2–4 players whose combined skills match what the town currently needs. A solo player gets matched to a complementary teammate, not a clone.

Flex slot

One squad slot is always a wildcard — any speciality welcome. This prevents queue starvation for rare roles and lets curious players try a new speciality in low-stakes co-op before committing to rank it up.
