Core game loop for the solarpunk crafting game

Session loop (per visit)

1
Arrive at a town

Travel between towns on a craftable solar bike, train, or glider depending on your tier. The journey is short but scenic — loading screen replaced by a mini travel moment.

↓
2
Read the restoration board

The town's needs are displayed: broken panels on the south roof, collapsed irrigation ditch, empty seed library. You pick tasks that match your speciality or queue for a matched squad.

↓
3
Gather, craft, install

Forage or salvage materials nearby. Craft the needed component at a workbench. Install it in the world — the broken thing visually transforms. Other players see your fix immediately.

↓
4
Town health ticks up

A visible progress bar on the town fills. Hit milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) to trigger collective celebrations — the town lights up, NPCs throw a small festival, new areas unlock.

↓
5
Earn rank + move on

Completing tasks earns XP in your active speciality. Hit rank thresholds to unlock harder quests, new towns, and better crafting recipes. Return to any town you've been to, any time.

What makes it feel solarpunk

A
Nothing is destroyed — only fixed

No combat, no villains. The "enemy" is entropy and neglect. Quests are always constructive: repair, restore, plant, connect. This holds the cozy tone without being saccharine.

B
The world visibly changes

Restored towns look different — solar panels gleam on rooftops, gardens bloom, lights glow at night. Players who return after weeks away see the town transformed. That's the emotional payoff.

C
Skills feel grounded and real

Sparkswright tasks teach real concepts — panel angle matters, battery chemistry affects storage. Grower quests involve real crop companions. Light educational layer; never preachy.

D
Community is the meta-game

A fully restored town unlocks a player-run market and communal build space. Veterans return to trade with new arrivals. The "end game" is becoming a mentor in a thriving place you helped build.

AI
Where AI adds real value

NPC town elders remember your previous visits and comment on your work. An AI Roblox assistant can procedurally generate quest variants so no two Tidewarden jobs feel identical. Keeps replay value high without heavy content dev.
